## The word

**Bountiful** — my human harvests bounties the way a field is harvested:
every free one worked deeply, small wins gathered until they add up.
A real word (abundant, generous) and a wry pun on the bounty hunt itself.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single word to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–30
characters) and validates the length before writing.
